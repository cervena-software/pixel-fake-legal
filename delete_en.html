Delete your Pixel Fake account

App: Pixel Fake  ·  Developed by: Cervena Software  ·  Last updated: November 2026

You can delete your Pixel Fake account and its associated data in two ways:
  • In the app: deletion is immediate.
  • Without the app installed: by sending us an email.

1. Before you start: cancel your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription or issue a refund. Subscriptions are billed by the store (Google Play or the App Store), not by us. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in the store first so it does not renew:
  • Google Play: at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions (Google help).
  • App Store: in your Apple Account settings (Apple help).
If you need help with this step, email us at cervenasoftware@gmail.com.

2. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Pixel Fake and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → My account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm the deletion. For security, the app may ask you to sign in again before confirming.

Your account and its associated data are deleted immediately, and your analysis history is removed from that device.

If you only want to delete your analysis history and keep your account, you can do so from Settings or from the History screen.

3. Delete your account without the app installed

  1. Email cervenasoftware@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Pixel Fake account”.
  2. Send it from the email address of your account (if you sign in with Google, from that Google email address). If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm that you own the account before deleting anything. This keeps anyone from deleting someone else's account.

We will confirm that we received your request and complete the deletion within 5 business days, as required by Section 16 of Argentina's Personal Data Protection Act (Law No. 25,326). We will email you once it is done.

Through this channel we cannot reach your phone. The analysis history stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or when you delete it in the app.

4. What data is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication): email address, encrypted password and, if you signed in with Google, the link to your Google account.
  • Your account record on our server: email address, free trial and subscription status with their dates, analysis counters, sign-up and terms-acceptance dates, and your usage data preference.
  • Usage control records that limit the number of analyses per minute and per hour.
  • Your analysis history (score, date and thumbnails), which exists only on your device. See section 3 if you request deletion by email.

5. What data is kept and for how long

DataWhy it is keptPeriod
Analysis result cache: the image's digital fingerprint (SHA-256) and the result. It does not include your identifier, your email address or the image. It avoids re-analyzing an image that was already analyzed. It is not linked to your account. 30 days after it was created; it is then deleted automatically (usually within the following 24 hours).
Server technical logs (may include your user identifier, the image fingerprint and the result). Security, abuse prevention and error diagnosis. 30 days.
Backups of the account system (Firebase Authentication, by Google). Managed by our provider; they are not used to restore deleted accounts. Up to 180 days.
Crash diagnostic data (Firebase Crashlytics), only if you agreed to share it. It is tied to a random installation identifier, not to your account, so we cannot locate it from your email address. 90 days.
Usage data (Google Analytics for Firebase), only if you agreed to share it. Same as above: it is not linked to your account. Up to 14 months.
Purchase records in Google Play or the App Store. The store handles the payment and keeps these records under its own policies and legal obligations. We cannot delete them. According to each store's policy.
Purchase history in RevenueCat, our subscription management provider, linked to a technical identifier (not to your email address). Payment management and fraud prevention. If you want us to delete it too, request it by email (section 3) and we will do so within the same period. Until you request its deletion.

The images you analyzed are never stored on our servers, so there are no images to delete.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about deleting your account or your data, email us at cervenasoftware@gmail.com. For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
