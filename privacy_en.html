Privacy Policy

App: Pixel Fake  ·  Developed by: Cervena Software  ·  Last updated: November 2026

Plain-language summary: Pixel Fake analyzes images to estimate whether they were AI-generated. Images you submit for analysis are never stored on our servers. We only collect the minimum data needed to run the app. If you choose not to share usage data, the app works exactly the same. You can delete your account and your data at any time.

2. What Data We Collect and Why

2.1 Account Data

To create your account we need your email address and a password. If you choose Sign in with Google, instead of a password Google shares your email address and your basic Google profile information (name and photo, if you have set them) with us. This data is stored in Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). Your password is never stored in plain text. We use this data only to identify you and protect your account.

2.2 Subscription Data

Subscriptions and payments are handled through Google Play or the Apple App Store, with the help of RevenueCat Inc. To link your purchase to your account, RevenueCat receives a technical identifier (not your email address). We receive confirmation of your subscription status (active, cancelled, expired, etc.), but we never have access to your credit card or payment details: that data is handled exclusively by Google or Apple.

2.3 Images Analyzed

Images you submit for analysis are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, which forwards them to the Sightengine service (sightengine.com) for classification. Original images are not stored on our servers.

The only thing we keep is a cryptographic fingerprint (SHA-256) of the image together with the analysis result, in a cache that is not linked to your account, for 30 days. It lets us avoid analyzing the same image twice. The image cannot be reconstructed from this fingerprint.

2.4 Analysis History (on your device)

Your analysis history (score, date and, if the thumbnail option is turned on, a small thumbnail) is stored only on your device. We do not sync it to any server. It is removed when you delete it in the app, when you sign out, when you delete your account in the app or when you uninstall the app.

2.5 Usage and Diagnostic Data (optional)

Only if you explicitly agree during onboarding or in Settings, we collect usage data (for example, which features you use or how many analyses you run) through Google Analytics for Firebase, and crash diagnostic data through Firebase Crashlytics. This data is tied to a random installation identifier, not to your account or your email address, and it does not include your images. We use it only to improve the app. If you do not agree, we collect no usage or diagnostic data at all. You can change this preference at any time in Settings.

2.6 Technical Operating Data

For the service to work, our server keeps an account record with your user identifier (generated by Firebase), your email address, your free trial and subscription status with their dates, the number of analyses in the current period, and your sign-up and terms-acceptance dates. It also records timestamps of your requests from the last hour to limit their frequency, and it produces technical logs (which may include your user identifier, the image fingerprint and the result) for security and error diagnosis.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the AI-generated image detection service.
  • To manage your account, your subscription and your free trial.
  • To enforce fair use of the service (monthly analysis limit and rate limiting).
  • To protect the service against fraud and abuse.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To improve the app through usage data, only if you have given your consent.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes. The app shows no ads.

4. Service Providers That Process Data on Our Behalf

We use the following service providers to operate the app:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Analytics, Crashlytics and Sign in with Google): accounts, server, service protection and, if you agree, usage and diagnostic data. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Sightengine: image analysis. It receives the image only to classify it and, according to its policy, deletes it once processed. Privacy policy: sightengine.com/policies/privacy
  • RevenueCat: subscription management. Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google LLC (Google Play) / Apple Inc. (App Store): payment processing and app distribution. Their privacy policies apply to transactions made in their stores.

These providers may store and process data outside Argentina (for example, in the United States or the European Union). We only work with providers that apply security measures and contractual data protection commitments.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data and account record: for as long as your account is active. When you delete it, it is removed from our systems immediately; backups of our provider's account system (Firebase Authentication) are deleted within up to 180 days.
  • Analysis cache (fingerprint and result, not linked to your account): 30 days after it was created; it is then deleted automatically.
  • Server technical logs: 30 days.
  • Rate-limiting records: the last hour; they are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Local history: until you delete it, sign out, delete your account in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Diagnostic data (Crashlytics), if you agreed to it: 90 days.
  • Usage data (Analytics), if you agreed to it: up to 14 months.
  • Purchase data: Google Play and the App Store keep it under their own policies. RevenueCat keeps your purchase history until you request its deletion.

6. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → My account → Delete my account), or without the app installed by emailing us from your account's email address. The steps, what is deleted and what is kept are explained in Delete your Pixel Fake account. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in the store first.

7. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and your data (see section 6).
  • Export your account data in JSON format from Settings → My account.
  • Withdraw your consent to usage and diagnostic data at any time in Settings.
  • Object to the processing of your data by contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at cervenasoftware@gmail.com.

Argentina's Agency for Access to Public Information (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública), as the supervisory authority under Law No. 25,326, handles complaints and claims from anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with personal data protection rules.

8. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data: all communications use HTTPS/TLS; secret service keys (for example, Sightengine's) live only on our server, never in the app; data access is protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules; and we use Firebase App Check to verify that requests come from the legitimate app.

9. Children's Privacy

Pixel Fake is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to minors. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.
